# Content requirements — what to send us for SEO + AEO

_Last updated 2026-09-04. Companion to `docs/PENDING-WORK.md`._

This is the list of **raw material only you can provide**. Everything here feeds
search (SEO) and AI answers (AEO). You don't need to write anything polished —
**talk it through in a voice note or a Loom, or type rough bullet points**, and we
turn it into on-brand, human-sounding pages. The more specific and first-hand it
is, the better it ranks and the more often it gets cited.

## Why this matters (the 30-second version)

- **SEO** rewards depth, specificity and genuine expertise (Google's E-E-A-T).
  Generic copy that could be on any agency site doesn't rank. Yours can, because
  you have real accounts, real numbers and a real point of view.
- **AEO** (getting quoted by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini)
  rewards **original facts, clear definitions and named sources**. An AI won't
  cite "we drive great results". It will happily cite "brands in fashion on
  Shopify in India typically see X", if you're the one who said it first.
- The single most valuable thing you can give us is a **real number with a
  source and permission to publish it.** One of those beats a page of adjectives.

## How to send it

1. Pick an item below.
2. Record yourself answering the prompts (phone voice memo or Loom is perfect),
   or type bullets. Ramble — we'll edit.
3. Drop the file/notes in the shared folder (or paste in Slack) and tag which
   section it's for.
4. For anything with a **client name or number, add one line of permission**:
   "You can publish [brand], [metric], [period] on the website." We publish
   nothing attributed without that.

## Priority 1 — Case studies (the biggest single win) 🔴

We currently publish three headline results (₹4.43 Cr / ₹14 L / ₹10 L) but have
**no detailed case-study pages** behind them. Detailed, permissioned case studies
are the strongest SEO and AEO asset you have. Target: 6–8 at launch.

For **each** client you're willing to name, record answers to:

- Brand name, category, where they sell (India / UAE), and their site.
- The situation when they came to you (in their words if you have it).
- What you actually did, in order (the first three changes that mattered).
- The **before → after numbers**, with the period and what drove them:
  - ROAS / MER, CAC, AOV, conversion rate, monthly revenue, ad spend.
- One or two things that went wrong or surprised you (this reads as real, and
  it's what founders search for).
- A **client quote**, even a rough one from WhatsApp, with permission to use it.
- Written permission to publish the name + numbers.

> Even **two** fully-detailed, permissioned case studies unlock the
> `/case-studies/[brand]/` pages, richer schema, and honest proof no competitor
> in your SERP is showing.

## Priority 2 — Real sales-call objections and questions 🔴

The FAQs on the site are good, but the highest-converting, best-ranking FAQs use
the **exact words founders actually say**. This is gold for AEO because it matches
how people phrase questions to an AI.

- Send us **20–30 real questions/objections** you hear on discovery calls, in the
  founder's own phrasing. Rough list is fine. Examples of the kind of thing:
  - "How is this different from the agency I just fired?"
  - "Why can't you just guarantee me a 4x ROAS?"
  - "Do I have to give you my ad account passwords?"
  - "What happens if it doesn't work in month one?"
- For each, a sentence or two of how you actually answer it.

We'll turn these into FAQ blocks (with FAQ schema) across the relevant pages.

## Priority 3 — Original data and benchmarks (pure AEO fuel) 🟠

This is what gets you **cited by AI** and linked by other sites. You sit on data
nobody else publishes. Even ranges are fine ("typically", "in our experience").

Anything you can share, per category (fashion / beauty / home / wellness /
jewellery) and per market (India / UAE), for example:

- Typical break-even ROAS, CAC, AOV and MER ranges you see.
- Typical Meta vs Google split, or CPMs/CPCs you see in India vs UAE.
- What a "good" Shopify conversion rate or LCP looks like in your book.
- Seasonality notes (Ramadan/DSF in UAE, festive in India, etc.).
- "X out of the last Y stores we audited had [specific problem]."

Send it however you have it — a screenshot of a sheet, a voice note, whatever.
We'll frame each as a clearly-labelled, first-party observation (never invented,
never dressed up as a study it isn't).

## Priority 4 — Founder / expert point of view per service 🟠

Google and AI models both reward content with a **named, credible author**. Right
now the pages are strong but unattributed. A short POV per pillar, from whichever
founder owns it, does two things: it makes the page an expert's page, and it
gives us author bylines and `Person` schema.

For each service (or the ones you care most about), the owning founder records
3–5 minutes on:

- The one thing most brands get wrong about this, and what you do instead.
- A short story of a time it worked (or blew up) — no names needed if sensitive.
- Your actual opinion on a live debate (Advantage+ vs manual, SEO vs paid,
  custom vs template, etc.).

## Priority 5 — How you actually run things (methodology) 🟡

Unique process = unique content that can't be copied. For each pillar, a rough
walk-through of your real workflow:

- Your creative testing pipeline, step by step (what a "test cell" is to you,
  how you read a winner, how fast you cut a loser).
- Your audit checklist (what you look at first in an account or a store).
- Your reporting rhythm (what's in the monthly read, what you deliberately leave
  out).

## Priority 6 — Glossary definitions with rupee examples 🟡

The `/glossary/` pages are stubbed and set to publish ~25 metric definitions
(`DefinedTerm` schema — strong AEO). We can draft these, but they're far better
with **your** worked example numbers. For each term (ROAS, CAC, AOV, MER,
contribution margin, break-even ROAS, LTV, payback, CPM, CTR, CPC, CPP, blended
vs platform ROAS, etc.):

- A one-line plain definition in your words.
- A worked example **in rupees** from a real (or realistic) brand.

## Priority 7 — Teardown material 🟡

Teardowns are your positioning differentiator (`/teardowns/`). To publish the
first few, send per brand:

- Which public D2C brand to tear down (their store + a few of their public ads).
- 5–10 things you'd change and why, roughly (we'll write it up constructively).
- Confirmation you're happy to publish a public, named-but-fair critique.

## The fastest path, if you only do one thing

Record a **20-minute voice note** walking through your two best client results
end to end — the situation, what you did, the before/after numbers, and a line of
permission. That single recording unlocks two case-study pages, a batch of real
FAQs, and several original data points, which is more SEO + AEO value than any
amount of copy polishing we can do on our own.
