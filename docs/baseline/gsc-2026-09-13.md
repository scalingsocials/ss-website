## What this baseline says about the migration

**Branded search is the business.** `scaling socials` and its variants are ~76%
of 28-day clicks, at position 2.2. Brand queries recover fastest after a
migration because the homepage is the target and it keeps its URL — so a brand
position drop would be the first real alarm.

**Non-brand is nearly all impressions, almost no clicks.** `performance marketing
agency bangalore` alone drew 603 impressions in 90 days for **1 click** at
position 35.7. There is a large pool of commercial demand the old site ranks for
but cannot convert from page 4. That is the headroom the new site is built for,
and it is why a short-term click dip would be misleading — clicks were never
coming from these terms anyway.

**Watch `/careers/`.** 18 clicks and 427 impressions in 28 days, and it is the
only ranking URL that redirects **off-domain** to `careers.scalingsocials.com`.
Cross-domain redirects pass equity less reliably; if the rankings you care about
include careers, verify that one first.

**`/performance-marketing-agency-bangalore/` is the one to protect.** 1,210
impressions in 28 days, the highest of any page, and it keeps its URL. Any change
to its title, H1 or content is a live experiment on your biggest impression pool.
