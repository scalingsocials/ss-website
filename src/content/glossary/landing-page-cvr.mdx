Landing page conversion rate is where most paid media is quietly lost, and it is usually cheaper to fix than the ad account. The traffic is already bought; every improvement here compounds across every campaign you run.

The first diagnostic is the gap between link clicks and landing page views. If Meta reports 10,000 clicks and only 7,000 landing page views, three thousand people left before the page finished loading. No creative change fixes that — it is a speed problem, and on Indian mobile networks it is a very common one.

After speed comes match. An ad promising a specific product that lands on a homepage forces the visitor to search for what they were shown, and a meaningful share simply leave. Then trust: visible delivery timelines, return policy, payment options and real reviews all do measurable work at Indian D2C price points. These are unglamorous fixes, but moving a page from 1.5% to 2% cuts cost per purchase by a quarter with no extra media spend at all.
