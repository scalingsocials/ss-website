Advantage+ Shopping is Meta's answer to the fact that manual audience targeting has steadily lost its edge. You supply the creative, the catalogue and the budget, and Meta decides who sees what. For a lot of Indian D2C catalogues that trade works well.

The honest position is that it is neither a shortcut nor a gimmick. It reduces the number of decisions you make about targeting and increases the number that matter about creative, because creative volume and quality become the main lever you still control. Accounts that do well on it are usually the ones feeding it a genuine variety of angles rather than five versions of the same image.

Where it struggles is narrow catalogues, very high consideration purchases, and brands with strong existing-customer overlap that needs excluding. We run it where it beats a manual structure on the same window and stay manual where control pays for itself, which is a decision to make per account rather than a philosophy to adopt.
